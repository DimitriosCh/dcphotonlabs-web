2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect the following:

2.1 Account information

  • If you sign in with Google: your name, email address, and profile photo (as provided by Google).
  • If you create an email/password account: your email address and authentication credentials (passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are not stored by us in plain text).

2.2 Fitness profile and preferences

  • Fitness level and training goals
  • Preferred workout styles and focus areas
  • Trip length / itinerary settings you enter
  • Subscription or premium status related to your account

2.3 Photos and equipment context

  • Photos you take or select of gym equipment or training spaces, used to help identify available equipment and generate a suitable workout.
  • Related context you provide (for example, notes about available gear).

2.4 Workout content

  • Generated workout plans and exercise details
  • Workout history saved to your account
  • Regeneration / lock-and-swap choices when you use premium features

2.5 Usage and technical data

  • Basic technical information needed to run the App securely (for example, authentication tokens, app requests to our backend, and usage counters such as free workout-generation credits and daily equipment-scan, regeneration and Directory-sharing limits)
  • App integrity signals: the App uses Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity to confirm that requests to our backend come from a genuine, unmodified copy of the App. This attests the app and device integrity; it is not used to identify or track you.
  • Basic on-device app state needed to run the App on your device

2.6 Purchases (when enabled)

  • If you subscribe through Google Play, purchase and entitlement status may be processed by Google Play Billing and our subscription partner (for example, RevenueCat). We receive confirmation of entitlement status so we can unlock Premium features. We do not receive your full payment card number.

2.7 Location (optional)

  • When you save a hotel gym to My Hotel Scans, you can choose to attach your current location so you can find that gym again later. This is off by default and is asked for each time you save.
  • If you turn it on and grant location permission, the App reads your device’s location once, at the moment you save, and stores the latitude and longitude with that saved gym. We do not track your location in the background or over time.
  • If you leave it off, deny permission, or location is unavailable, the gym is saved without a location.

2.8 Hotel Gym Directory contributions and reports (optional)

  • You can choose to share a saved hotel gym to the public Hotel Gym Directory so other travelers can see what equipment a hotel has. Sharing is off by default and happens only when you confirm it.
  • A contribution contains the hotel name, city, country (optional), the equipment list and, if you attached a location to that saved gym, an approximate location (rounded to roughly 100 meters). It never includes your photos.
  • Contributions are reviewed before they are published. We keep your user ID with the contribution so we can review it, prevent abuse and honor deletion requests; it is never published.
  • If you report a Directory entry, we store the entry, the reason, any details you write, and your user ID so we can review the report.

3. How we use your information

We use the information above to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Generate personalized workout plans
  • Save and show your workout history
  • Enforce free-tier limits and Premium features
  • Review, publish and maintain the Hotel Gym Directory, including handling reports about its entries
  • Operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the App
  • Respond to support and privacy requests
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

4. AI processing

To generate workouts, we send relevant inputs (such as fitness preferences, trip details, identified equipment, and—when you provide them—equipment photos or derived equipment lists) to our backend services and to third-party AI infrastructure providers (currently including OpenRouter and the underlying model providers it routes to).

These providers process the data to return workout recommendations. Do not upload photos or text that contain sensitive personal information you do not want processed for this purpose.

Locations saved with your hotel gyms (see 2.7) are not sent to AI providers.

5. How we share information

We share information only with service providers that help us operate the App, including:

  • Google Firebase (authentication, cloud database, cloud functions / backend processing)
  • OpenRouter / AI model providers (workout generation)
  • Google Play (app distribution and, when enabled, billing)
  • RevenueCat (when enabled, subscription entitlement management)

Hotel Gym Directory. Hotel gym information you choose to share (hotel name, city, country, equipment list and approximate location) is shown to other signed-in users of the App once reviewed, together with the month it was last verified. It is never shown with your name, email address or user ID.

These providers process data on our behalf or as independent controllers where applicable under their own terms and privacy policies. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business transfer (for example, a merger or acquisition).

6. Data storage and retention

  • Account profile data, workout history, saved hotel gyms (including any location you chose to attach), and related cloud data are stored in Firebase (Firestore) associated with your user ID. Your saved hotel gyms are visible only to you.
  • You can delete an individual saved hotel gym, and its location, at any time from Profile → My Hotel Scans.
  • We retain account data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the App and meet legal requirements.
  • If you request deletion, we will delete or anonymize personal data we control, except where we must retain information for legitimate legal or security reasons.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted transport (HTTPS) and access controls for backend services. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Receive a portable copy of your data

  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise these rights, email dc.photon.labs@gmail.com from the address associated with your account and tell us what you need. You can also stop using the App and sign out at any time.

If you signed in with Google, you can also manage Google account permissions in your Google Account settings.

9. Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your TravelFitAI account and associated personal data at any time. This includes your profile, preferences, saved workouts, and saved hotel gyms (including any saved locations).

In the App

  1. Open TravelFitAI and sign in
  2. Go to Profile (account icon)
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm

Deleting your account in the App permanently removes your account, saved workouts, saved hotel gyms (including any saved locations), usage counters, and any Directory contributions still awaiting review. Hotel gym information already published in the Directory stays there, but your user ID is removed from it and from any reports you made, so it is no longer linked to you.

By email

If you cannot use the in-app option, email us from the address linked to your account:

dc.photon.labs@gmail.com

Please use the subject line “Delete my TravelFitAI account” and include the email address for the account you want deleted. We will process verified requests and delete your account data, except where we must retain limited information for legitimate legal or security reasons.

Reporting Directory content

To report incorrect, misleading or inappropriate information in the Hotel Gym Directory, tap the flag (Report an issue) on the entry in the App, or email dc.photon.labs@gmail.com with the hotel name and city. We review reports and correct or remove entries where appropriate.

10. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

11. International processing

We may process information in the European Union and other countries where our service providers operate. If you use the App from another country, your information may be transferred to and processed in those locations.

ReceiptoAI — Privacy Policy

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how ReceiptoAI ("the Service"), including our Android app, web app at receiptoai.com, and related backend services, collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Service. The Service is operated by DC Photon Labs (Dimitrios Christaras L.P.) ("we", "us", "our").

Data We Collect

To scan, organize, and report on your travel receipts, we collect:

  • Account data: email address, authentication identifiers (including when you sign in with email/password or Google Sign-In), display name, language and currency preferences, and onboarding choices.
  • Tax & profile data: optional tax identification numbers you provide (such as Greek ΑΦΜ / EU VAT ID), account type (personal or business), and optional signature name fields.
  • Receipt content: images and PDFs you capture, upload, or forward, including travel documents such as boarding passes and train tickets (which can show your name, route, flight or train number, and seat); data extracted from them (merchant name, date, line items, amounts, currency, category, merchant tax IDs found on receipts); and edits you make.
  • Email-in data: if you use email forwarding, we assign you a personal forwarding address (receipts+<alias>@receipts.dcphotonlabs.com) and process inbound messages you send there, including sender address, subject, message body or plain-text content, attachments, and delivery metadata needed to create receipts.
  • Expense & trip data: trip names, date ranges, tags, tag groups, budgets, and expense reports (including generated PDF/CSV exports).
  • Signature image: optional PNG signature you upload for PDF reports, stored in our cloud storage.
  • Organization / B2B data: if you redeem a company license code, we store the organization identifier, your seat membership, and license status linked to your account.
  • Subscription & purchase data: whether you have Premium, the plan you bought (monthly or yearly), the store it was bought through (Google Play or web), its renewal and expiry dates, and whether there is a billing problem. We receive this from our subscription manager, RevenueCat, linked to your account by an anonymous account identifier. We never receive or store your full card or bank details; payments are handled by Google Play or, for web purchases, by Stripe.
  • Usage data: monthly and daily AI scan counts, last data-export timestamp, and app interactions needed to operate features, enforce plan limits, and prevent abuse.
  • Currency preferences: your chosen home currency, and exchange rates or card-charged amounts used to convert receipt totals into it.
  • Crash reports (Android app): when the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, Firebase Crashlytics records the error and where it happened in the app's code (stack trace), the app state at that moment, point-in-time device information (model, operating system version, orientation, free memory and disk space), the app version, and a random Crashlytics installation identifier used to count how many devices are affected. Crash reports do not include your receipts, extracted amounts, or account email.
  • Technical & device data: device model, operating system, browser type (web), locale, crash/diagnostic logs, and IP-derived metadata processed by our infrastructure providers for security and reliability.

Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction requiring a legal basis, we process personal data as follows:

  • Performance of a contract — to create and maintain your account; scan, store, tag, and organize receipts; generate reports and exports; operate email-in forwarding; and sell, bill, and provide the Premium subscription you buy.
  • Legitimate interests — to secure the Service, prevent fraud and abuse, enforce our terms, improve reliability, and maintain accurate subscription limits, balanced against your rights.
  • Legal obligation — where we must retain or disclose information to comply with applicable law.
  • Consent — only where we explicitly ask for consent (for example, optional permissions you can revoke in device or browser settings). You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

Third-Party AI & OCR Processing

ReceiptoAI uses OpenRouter to route receipt extraction requests to third-party artificial intelligence and OCR models (currently Google Gemini). Receipt images, PDFs, and extracted or forwarded text are transmitted to these providers strictly to perform extraction. Every request instructs OpenRouter to use only providers that do not retain the request after answering it (zero data retention) and do not use it for training. We do not use your receipt content to train AI models, and we do not sell receipt data to third parties.

Service Providers (Processors)

We use trusted providers that process data on our behalf, including:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud — authentication, database (Firestore), file storage, serverless functions, and hosting (including receiptoai.com).
  • Google Sign-In — optional account sign-in on mobile and web.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google) — crash and error reporting for the Android app, so we can find and fix problems.
  • OpenRouter — AI inference routing for receipt extraction (see above).
  • Resend — inbound email receipt forwarding at receipts.dcphotonlabs.com.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management: records your Premium purchases from Google Play and the web, and tells us whether your subscription is active.
  • Stripe — payment processing for subscriptions bought on the web (card details are entered on Stripe's or RevenueCat's checkout pages, never on ours).
  • Google Play — billing for subscriptions bought in the Android app, under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

These providers act as processors under our instructions. Some may process data in the United States or other countries outside the European Economic Area. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and/or equivalent transfer mechanisms offered by our providers.

ReceiptoAI, de DC Photon Labs (Dimitrios Christaras L.P.) · English version

Puedes eliminar tu cuenta de ReceiptoAI y todos sus datos en cualquier momento, desde la app, desde la web o pidiéndonoslo. No necesitas tener la app instalada.

Opción 1: en la app web (sin la app)

  1. Ve a receiptoai.com e inicia sesión con la cuenta que quieres eliminar.
  2. Abre Ajustes y desplázate hasta el final.
  3. Elige Eliminar cuenta y confirma.

Opción 2: en la app para Android

  1. Abre ReceiptoAI y toca tu Perfil.
  2. Desplázate hacia abajo, toca Eliminar cuenta y confirma.

Opción 3: pídenoslo

Si ya no puedes iniciar sesión, escribe a dc.photon.labs@gmail.com con el asunto «ReceiptoAI account deletion» desde el correo de la cuenta. Te lo confirmaremos y la eliminaremos en un plazo de 30 días.

Qué se elimina

Tu perfil y tu cuenta de acceso, todos los recibos y sus imágenes, los datos extraídos, los viajes, las etiquetas, los informes de gastos y sus PDF, las imágenes de firma, las exportaciones de datos, tu dirección de reenvío por correo y tu pertenencia a organizaciones. La eliminación es definitiva y no se puede deshacer: si quieres una copia, exporta antes tus datos desde Ajustes.

Qué se conserva y durante cuánto tiempo

  • Copias de seguridad: las copias cifradas de la base de datos que aún contengan tus datos se sobrescriben en su rotación habitual, en un plazo máximo de 8 semanas.
  • Informes de fallos (Firebase Crashlytics): se eliminan automáticamente a los 90 días.
  • Compras: eliminar tu cuenta no cancela la suscripción; cancélala antes en Google Play. Google Play, Stripe y RevenueCat conservan los registros de compra según lo exigido por motivos fiscales, contables y de prevención del fraude, conforme a sus propias políticas; puedes pedirnos que solicitemos la eliminación del registro de RevenueCat vinculado a tu cuenta.

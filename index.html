Last updated: September 1, 2026 · Operated by DC Photon Labs (Dimitrios Christaras L.P.)

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how TravelFitAI ("the App"), operated by DC Photon Labs (Dimitrios Christaras L.P.) ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, and protects information when you use the App.

Data Collection

We collect the minimum data required to generate and personalize your workout plans:

  • Account data: email address and authentication identifiers, if you create an account.
  • Fitness profile data: fitness level, goals, available equipment, injuries or limitations you choose to disclose, and trip details (destination, dates, hotel/gym availability) you enter to generate a plan.
  • Usage data: app interactions, generated plans, and crash/diagnostic logs used to improve reliability.
  • Device data: device model, OS version, and locale, collected automatically for compatibility and support purposes.

Third-Party AI APIs

TravelFitAI uses third-party artificial intelligence providers to generate workout recommendations. Data you submit to generate a plan (such as fitness goals, equipment, and trip details) may be transmitted to these AI service providers strictly to produce your workout output. This data is processed under each provider's own data-handling terms and is not used by us to train third-party foundation models. We do not knowingly send government IDs, financial account numbers, or precise health/medical records to AI providers.

Device Camera & Photos Permissions

TravelFitAI may request access to your device's camera or photo library only if you choose to attach an optional progress photo or profile picture to your account. This access is opt-in, used solely for the feature you invoke, and is never activated in the background. You can deny or revoke this permission at any time in your device settings without losing access to core workout-generation features.

How We Use Your Data

  • To generate and personalize your workout plans.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the App.
  • To provide customer support and respond to inquiries.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, or abuse.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with: (a) AI service providers as described above, strictly to generate your requested content; (b) infrastructure providers (hosting, analytics, crash reporting) acting on our behalf; and (c) authorities where required by law.

Data Retention

We retain account and fitness profile data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the App's features. You may request deletion at any time as described below.

User Rights

Depending on your location (including under GDPR and CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.

  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at dc.photon.labs@gmail.com. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

TravelFitAI is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

We use industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

ReceiptoAI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026 · Operated by DC Photon Labs (Dimitrios Christaras L.P.)

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how ReceiptoAI ("the Service"), including our Android app, web app at receiptoai.com, and related backend services, collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Service. The Service is operated by DC Photon Labs (Dimitrios Christaras L.P.) ("we", "us", "our").

Data We Collect

To scan, organize, and report on your travel receipts, we collect:

  • Account data: email address, authentication identifiers (including when you sign in with email/password or Google Sign-In), display name, language and currency preferences, and onboarding choices.
  • Tax & profile data: optional tax identification numbers you provide (such as Greek ΑΦΜ / EU VAT ID), account type (personal or business), and optional signature name fields.
  • Receipt content: images and PDFs you capture, upload, or forward; data extracted from them (merchant name, date, line items, amounts, currency, category, merchant tax IDs found on receipts); and edits you make.
  • Email-in data: if you use email forwarding, we assign you a personal forwarding address (receipts+<alias>@receipts.dcphotonlabs.com) and process inbound messages you send there, including sender address, subject, message body or plain-text content, attachments, and delivery metadata needed to create receipts.
  • Expense & trip data: trip names, date ranges, tags, tag groups, budgets, and expense reports (including generated PDF/CSV exports).
  • Signature image: optional PNG signature you upload for PDF reports, stored in our cloud storage.
  • Organization / B2B data: if you redeem a company license code, we store the organization identifier, your seat membership, and license status linked to your account.
  • Subscription & purchase data: whether you have Premium, the plan you bought (monthly or yearly), the store it was bought through (Google Play or web), its renewal and expiry dates, and whether there is a billing problem. We receive this from our subscription manager, RevenueCat, linked to your account by an anonymous account identifier. We never receive or store your full card or bank details; payments are handled by Google Play or, for web purchases, by Stripe.
  • Usage data: monthly and daily AI scan counts, last data-export timestamp, and app interactions needed to operate features, enforce plan limits, and prevent abuse.
  • Currency preferences: your chosen home currency, and exchange rates or card-charged amounts used to convert receipt totals into it.
  • Technical & device data: device model, operating system, browser type (web), locale, crash/diagnostic logs, and IP-derived metadata processed by our infrastructure providers for security and reliability.

Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction requiring a legal basis, we process personal data as follows:

  • Performance of a contract — to create and maintain your account; scan, store, tag, and organize receipts; generate reports and exports; operate email-in forwarding; and sell, bill, and provide the Premium subscription you buy.
  • Legitimate interests — to secure the Service, prevent fraud and abuse, enforce our terms, improve reliability, and maintain accurate subscription limits, balanced against your rights.
  • Legal obligation — where we must retain or disclose information to comply with applicable law.
  • Consent — only where we explicitly ask for consent (for example, optional permissions you can revoke in device or browser settings). You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

Third-Party AI & OCR Processing

ReceiptoAI uses OpenRouter to route receipt extraction requests to third-party artificial intelligence and OCR models (currently Google Gemini). Receipt images, PDFs, and extracted or forwarded text are transmitted to these providers strictly to perform extraction. Every request instructs OpenRouter to use only providers that do not retain the request after answering it (zero data retention) and do not use it for training. We do not use your receipt content to train AI models, and we do not sell receipt data to third parties.

Service Providers (Processors)

We use trusted providers that process data on our behalf, including:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud — authentication, database (Firestore), file storage, serverless functions, and hosting (including receiptoai.com).
  • Google Sign-In — optional account sign-in on mobile and web.
  • OpenRouter — AI inference routing for receipt extraction (see above).
  • Resend — inbound email receipt forwarding at receipts.dcphotonlabs.com.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management: records your Premium purchases from Google Play and the web, and tells us whether your subscription is active.
  • Stripe — payment processing for subscriptions bought on the web (card details are entered on Stripe's or RevenueCat's checkout pages, never on ours).
  • Google Play — billing for subscriptions bought in the Android app, under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

These providers act as processors under our instructions. Some may process data in the United States or other countries outside the European Economic Area. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and/or equivalent transfer mechanisms offered by our providers.

Device Camera, Photos & Files

ReceiptoAI requests access to your device's camera to photograph receipts, to your photo library to import existing images, and to local files for uploads. On the web app, you choose files from your browser. These permissions are used only when you initiate a scan or upload and are not used in the background for unrelated purposes. You can deny or revoke access in device or browser settings; some features (camera scan, gallery import, or email-in attachments you do not forward) may then be unavailable.

Cookies & Similar Technologies (Web App)

The web app at receiptoai.com uses strictly necessary authentication session mechanisms operated by Firebase Auth and may store functional preferences (such as language) in your browser's local storage. We do not use third-party advertising cookies on the Service. You can clear site data in your browser settings, which may sign you out or reset preferences.

How We Use Your Data

  • To extract, organize, tag, and search receipt and expense data on your behalf.
  • To process email you forward to your personal ReceiptoAI address and create receipts from attachments or message content.
  • To generate expense reports, PDF/CSV exports, and data exports you request.
  • To sell and manage subscriptions, license codes, and plan limits (monthly and daily AI scan limits).
  • To operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Service.
  • To provide customer support and respond to inquiries.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, or abuse.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with: (a) AI/OCR and email service providers as described above, strictly to provide the Service; (b) infrastructure, subscription, and payment providers acting on our behalf; and (c) authorities where required by law.

Data Retention

We retain receipts, expense data, exports, and account data for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service (for example, historical expense reports). When you delete your account (see below), we erase personal data from our active systems, including Firestore records, email-alias mappings, organization membership links, and files in cloud storage (receipt images, report PDFs, signature images, and prior export files). Residual copies in encrypted backups, if any, are removed on our normal backup rotation cycle (typically within 30 days).

Deleting your account does not cancel a paid subscription — cancel it first (see our Terms). Purchase and transaction records held by Google Play, Stripe, and RevenueCat are kept by them as required for tax, accounting, and fraud-prevention purposes under their own policies; you can ask us to request deletion of the RevenueCat record linked to your account.

  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Request a portable copy of your data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent where processing is consent-based.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

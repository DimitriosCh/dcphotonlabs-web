TravelFitAI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026 · Operated by DC Photon Labs (optEngineering LP)

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how TravelFitAI ("the App"), operated by DC Photon Labs (optEngineering LP) ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, and protects information when you use the App.

Data Collection

We collect the minimum data required to generate and personalize your workout plans:

  • Account data: email address and authentication identifiers, if you create an account.
  • Fitness profile data: fitness level, goals, available equipment, injuries or limitations you choose to disclose, and trip details (destination, dates, hotel/gym availability) you enter to generate a plan.
  • Usage data: app interactions, generated plans, and crash/diagnostic logs used to improve reliability.
  • Device data: device model, OS version, and locale, collected automatically for compatibility and support purposes.

Third-Party AI APIs

TravelFitAI uses third-party artificial intelligence providers to generate workout recommendations. Data you submit to generate a plan (such as fitness goals, equipment, and trip details) may be transmitted to these AI service providers strictly to produce your workout output. This data is processed under each provider's own data-handling terms and is not used by us to train third-party foundation models. We do not knowingly send government IDs, financial account numbers, or precise health/medical records to AI providers.

Device Camera & Photos Permissions

TravelFitAI may request access to your device's camera or photo library only if you choose to attach an optional progress photo or profile picture to your account. This access is opt-in, used solely for the feature you invoke, and is never activated in the background. You can deny or revoke this permission at any time in your device settings without losing access to core workout-generation features.

How We Use Your Data

  • To generate and personalize your workout plans.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the App.
  • To provide customer support and respond to inquiries.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, or abuse.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with: (a) AI service providers as described above, strictly to generate your requested content; (b) infrastructure providers (hosting, analytics, crash reporting) acting on our behalf; and (c) authorities where required by law.

Data Retention

We retain account and fitness profile data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the App's features. You may request deletion at any time as described below.

User Rights

Depending on your location (including under GDPR and CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Request a portable copy of your data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at dc.photon.labs@gmail.com. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

Children's Privacy

TravelFitAI is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Security

We use industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

ReceiptoAI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026 · Operated by DC Photon Labs (optEngineering LP)

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how ReceiptoAI ("the App"), operated by DC Photon Labs (optEngineering LP) ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, and protects information when you use the App.

Data Collection

To scan and organize your travel receipts, we collect:

  • Account data: email address and authentication identifiers, if you create an account.
  • Receipt content: images of receipts you capture or upload, and the data extracted from them (merchant name, date, line items, amounts, currency, and category).
  • Expense & trip data: trip names, budgets, and expense reports you create within the App.
  • Usage data: app interactions and crash/diagnostic logs used to improve reliability.
  • Device data: device model, OS version, and locale, collected automatically for compatibility and support purposes.

Third-Party AI APIs

ReceiptoAI uses third-party artificial intelligence and optical character recognition (OCR) providers to extract text and data from receipt images you capture or upload. Receipt images and extracted text may be transmitted to these providers strictly to perform this extraction and are processed under each provider's own data-handling terms. We do not use your receipt images to train third-party foundation models, and we do not sell receipt data to third parties.

Device Camera & Photos Permissions

ReceiptoAI requests access to your device's camera to let you photograph receipts directly, and to your photo library to let you import existing receipt images. These permissions are used exclusively to capture or select receipt images for scanning and are never accessed in the background or for any purpose unrelated to receipt scanning. You can deny or revoke camera and photo access at any time in your device settings; doing so will limit you to manual expense entry only.

How We Use Your Data

  • To extract and organize receipt and expense data on your behalf.
  • To generate expense reports and summaries you request.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the App.
  • To provide customer support and respond to inquiries.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, or abuse.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with: (a) AI/OCR service providers as described above, strictly to process your receipts; (b) infrastructure providers (hosting, analytics, crash reporting, cloud storage) acting on our behalf; and (c) authorities where required by law.

Data Retention

We retain receipts, expense data, and account data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the App's features (e.g., historical expense reports). You may request deletion at any time as described below.

User Rights

Depending on your location (including under GDPR and CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you, including stored receipts.
  • Request correction of inaccurate extracted data.
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Request a portable copy of your data (e.g., export of expense reports).
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at dc.photon.labs@gmail.com. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

Children's Privacy

ReceiptoAI is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Security

We use industry-standard technical and organizational measures, including encryption in transit, to protect your receipts and data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
